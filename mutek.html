Mutek

Blender2024

I created a 3D animated poster for the MUTEK music festival (fictional project), which specializes in experimental electronic music. My goal was to capture the atmosphere of the concerts through colorful reflections on water. The combination of neon lights and water creates a dynamic, immersive visual, offering a preview of the concert scenography.
