Flamingo
Liberation

Illustrator
After Effects2024

As part of a collaboration between La Poste and Libération, I designed a stamp illustrating a positive news story, printed in risograph with a digital animation. The animation is broken down into sequences, printed on an A3 sheet in Riso, scanned, and reassembled as an animated version.

My illustration focuses on the situation of Caribbean flamingos in the Dominican Republic, released back into the wild by associations. Rather than showing the landscape directly, I chose to symbolize the water as a mirror of the soul: a flamingo leans over the water, and its reflection appears brighter, illustrating the contrast between captivity and freedom.
