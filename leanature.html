Léa Nature

Illustrator
Figma
Photoshop2026

This project focuses on rethinking the brand identity of Léa Nature, a group bringing together 24 brands across different areas of everyday organic living. My aim was to address the challenges of brand recognition and transparency by making the parent company more visible to consumers, while preserving the individual identities of its sub-brands.

The visual concept is built around a stem-like line, which refers both to Léa Nature’s origins and its early focus on the benefits of plants, and to the idea of a common thread connecting all the brands through shared values. The colour palette reflects the diversity of the different sectors within the group.

The new identity is rolled out across various touchpoints, including logo animation, website design, the scenography of a pop-up store for Natexpo 2026, and the creation of branded stickers to be applied directly to existing product packaging. These stickers make it easier for consumers to identify products as part of the Léa Nature group, improving traceability and building consumer trust.
