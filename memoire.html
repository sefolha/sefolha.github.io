Evolution of brand identities

Indesign2026

My thesis focuses on the balance between modernization and continuity in the evolution of brand identities, especially in the food sector. In this work, I analyze creative strategies, especially rebranding, and how they are expressed visually. I explore how designers can use simpler shapes and color choices to keep a brand’s unique identity while also meeting modern communication needs.
