Tu es chez toi

Cap-Vert,
Afrique2024

This photographic series explores my connection to Cape Verde, my country of origin, where I do not live but where I feel I belong. Through my grandmother’s house in Santo Antão, her neighborhood, and Porto Novo, I express a sense of calm and belonging. I worked with a palette of warm, natural tones inspired by the landscape. My subjects are those that make up my daily life there: my family, the houses, and the street animals.
