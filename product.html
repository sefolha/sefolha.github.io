Ad Blossom gel

Blender2026

I created a series of 3D visuals as part of a fictional advertisement for the Blossom Gel by the brand Beauty of Joseon. My visual choices are based on the idea of freshness, as well as the product’s composition with plum blossoms. The ice also suggests the preservation of the active ingredients, and the flowers inside it strengthen the connection with the natural ingredient, as if it is frozen in a pure state.
