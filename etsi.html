Et si le luxe ? Chênaie

Photoshop
Camera Raw2026

I created an advertising campaign for Chênaie – Country Villas. It highlights the family-friendly and welcoming atmosphere of the place, inviting people to rent a cottage and come together to share simple moments.

The campaign was designed to be displayed in Paris metro stations, in order to reach active urban dwellers—couples or families living in Paris—seeking nature and quality time for a weekend getaway.
