New Day

Glyph
Illustrator2025

I designed a visual identity for the fictional brand NEW DAY, which specializes in upcycling and handmade products. As part of this, I created a typeface inspired by the safety pin. It represents the spirit of the brand: assembling, repairing, and giving materials a second life. The Pintype combines thin shapes, like the needle, with solid shapes, like the clasp, which allowed me to create a dynamic typeface, mainly intended for headlines.

I thought about the brand’s story: NEW DAY is aimed at a committed woman who loves upcycled clothing and appreciates patterns inspired by diverse cultures. I imagined how the identity would unfold in the brand’s communication, such as in an advertisement, standalone graphic patterns, or its own magazine.
