American Gigolo Opening credits
Liberation

After Effects
Illustrator2025

I reimagined the opening credits of the film *American Gigolo* (1980), aiming to convey the character’s downfall through the use of color. As in the film, the color palette gradually evolves, shifting from light tones to shades of red and darker hues as Julian’s (the main character) downfall becomes imminent. The minimalist, slightly pop-inspired graphic style, along with the music, draws on the visual and sound codes of the 1980s.
