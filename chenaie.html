Chênaie,
Les Domaines

Illustrator2026

Chênaie is a (fictional) company comprising two entities: “Chênaie – Country Villas”, which offers villas and country houses for stays, and “Chênaie – Event Houses”, a network of venues for rent throughout France. The goal was to design an identity that could be adapted to both universes while maintaining coherence. The brand needed to convey a luxurious and elegant image, but also a simple and natural one, in harmony with the many estates located in the countryside.
